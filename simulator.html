Stack-ranking performance-management simulator

Model how forced-ranking (firing the bottom performers), voluntary attrition, training, and hiring noise push a company's mean skill around over time. Everything runs in your browser — tweak the parameters and watch the outcome, or generate parameter sweeps like the charts at the bottom of the notebook.

← Read the write-up: No, you can't fire your way to a better company

Parameters

All skills are integers 1–5. The founding team is itself hired through the hiring bar, so year 0 already sits above the raw market mean.

Skill over time

Mean skill of the workforce across review cycles, for the parameters on the left. Updates live.

Parameter sweep

Pick a parameter to vary along the x-axis and (optionally) a second parameter to draw a line for each value. Everything else uses the settings on the left.

Ported from the stack_ranking.ipynb Monte-Carlo model. Open-source under GPL-3.0. The simulation is stochastic; the random seed makes any single run reproducible, and sweep repetitions average multiple seeds to smooth the lines.
